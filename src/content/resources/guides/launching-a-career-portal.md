A career portal only earns its keep when students return to it. This guide
walks through a rollout designed for adoption rather than a launch-day
announcement that nobody remembers by week two.

## 1. Start with one clear job

Pick a single, obvious reason for a student to open the portal in the first
week. A live vacancy that matches their programme works far better than a
generic welcome tour.

## 2. Seed real opportunities

An empty board teaches students there is nothing here. Before you invite
anyone, make sure there is genuine, relevant content waiting for them.

## 3. Close the loop

When a student applies, tell them what happens next. A visible status turns
a hopeful click into a process they trust and keep coming back to.

## 4. Report from day one

Track opens, applications and outcomes from the start. The first month of
data is what secures buy-in for the wider rollout.
