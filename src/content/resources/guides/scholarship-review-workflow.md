Scholarship season carries real weight. Behind every application is a
student hoping for a chance, and behind every decision is a reviewer trying
to be fair under time pressure. A clear workflow protects both.

## Define the criteria before the applications arrive

Fairness begins before the first submission. Agree on what you are
assessing and how it is scored, then make those criteria visible to every
reviewer so decisions rest on the same foundation.

## Assign reviewers deliberately

Spread the load and avoid conflicts of interest by assigning reviewers with
intention rather than letting applications pile onto whoever is available.

## Record the decision, not just the outcome

A stored rationale turns a difficult conversation into a documented one.
When an applicant asks why, you have a fair, consistent answer ready.
