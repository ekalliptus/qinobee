> This is an illustrative scenario. The institution and figures described
> here are fictional and are used to show how the platform is designed to
> work, not to represent a real customer.

## The situation

An imagined university launches a mentoring programme with enthusiasm, but by
mid-term many pairs have quietly stopped meeting. No one notices until the
end-of-year survey reveals the gap.

## The change

With a simple view of which pairs are active and which have gone quiet,
coordinators in this scenario can step in early. A short nudge at the right
moment keeps relationships alive.

## The illustrative result

In this imagined rollout, more pairs stay active through the full year, and
the programme's second-conversation rate becomes something the team can see
and protect rather than hope for.
