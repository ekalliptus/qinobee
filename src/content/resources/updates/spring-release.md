This quarter's release focuses on the moments where a few saved minutes add
up across a whole cohort.

## Cross-programme dashboards

Participation and outcome metrics from different programmes now sit in one
connected view, so leadership can read the whole picture without stitching
reports together by hand.

## Faster internship placements

Approval workflows have been streamlined to cut the back-and-forth between
students, supervisors and coordinators.

## Resume matching refinements

The resume tools now offer clearer, more actionable feedback when matching a
student's profile against a job description.
